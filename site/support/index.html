⚙️ HealthKit Permissions

To manage your health data permissions:

  • Go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health
  • Select "AskHealth AI"
  • Turn data types on or off as desired
  • Changes take effect immediately

🗑️ Data Deletion
