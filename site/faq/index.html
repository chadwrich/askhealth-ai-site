Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about AskHealth AI wellness coaching

⚠️ Important: AskHealth AI provides wellness coaching only, not medical advice. For medical questions, consult qualified healthcare professionals.

Privacy & Data Handling

How is my health data protected?

Your privacy is our top priority:

  • Apple HealthKit integration: Built on Apple's privacy-first platform
  • Your permission required: You control which data types to share
  • Temporary processing: Raw health data is not permanently stored
  • No data selling: We never sell your personal information
  • Revoke anytime: Change permissions through iOS Settings
What data does AskHealth AI access?

Only with your explicit permission, we may access:

  • Steps and daily activity
  • Sleep duration and patterns
  • Weight and body measurements
  • Calories consumed and burned
  • Heart rate data

You control exactly which data types to share.

Is my conversation data stored?

Yes, to provide continuous coaching:

  • Conversation history is stored to maintain context
  • This enables the AI to remember your wellness journey
  • Raw health metrics are processed temporarily, not stored
  • You can request data deletion by contacting support

Medical Advice Limitations

Does AskHealth AI provide medical advice?

No. AskHealth AI does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

We provide:

  • Wellness coaching and insights
  • Educational health information
  • General fitness and nutrition guidance
  • Motivation and encouragement

Always consult healthcare professionals for medical decisions.

Can I use this for medical emergencies?

No. For medical emergencies, call 911 immediately.

AskHealth AI is not designed to:

  • Detect medical emergencies
  • Provide emergency medical guidance
  • Replace emergency medical services
  • Diagnose urgent health conditions
What should I do for health concerns?

Consult qualified healthcare professionals for:

  • Medical advice and recommendations
  • Diagnosis of health conditions
  • Treatment decisions
  • Medication changes
  • Any health concerns or symptoms

AskHealth AI complements but never replaces professional medical care.

Apple HealthKit Integration

How does HealthKit integration work?

AskHealth AI integrates seamlessly with Apple Health:

  • Permission-based: You choose which data to share
  • Automatic sync: No manual data entry required
  • Real-time access: Get insights from current data
  • Device compatibility: Works with all HealthKit-compatible devices
Can I change my data permissions?

Yes, you have complete control:

  • Go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health
  • Select "AskHealth AI"
  • Turn data types on or off as desired
  • Changes take effect immediately
What if I don't have much health data?

AskHealth AI works with whatever data you have:

  • Even basic step counting provides valuable insights
  • More data enables more personalized coaching
  • You can add more data sources over time
  • The AI adapts to your available data

Technical Support

How do I get help with technical issues?

For technical support:

  • Visit our Support page for common solutions
  • Report issues through our GitHub repository
  • Include your device model and iOS version
  • Describe steps to reproduce the problem

We aim to respond within 2-3 business days.

What devices are supported?

AskHealth AI requires:

  • iOS device (iPhone or iPad)
  • iOS 14.0 or later
  • Apple Health app installed
  • HealthKit-compatible for best experience
