Overview

AskHealth AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to being transparent about how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the AskHealth AI mobile application.

Important: This app is currently in beta testing via Apple TestFlight. Some features are still under development.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you use AskHealth AI, we create and store:

  • User ID: A unique identifier for your account
  • Conversation ID: An identifier for maintaining conversation continuity across sessions

Conversation Data

To provide a continuous coaching experience, we store:

  • Conversation History: Your messages and AI responses
  • Conversation Summaries: Condensed versions of past conversations for context
  • Turn Counts: Number of interactions for usage tracking

Subscription and Entitlement Data

We track:

  • Entitlement State: Whether you are on free, subscribed, or canceled status
  • Subscription Status: For testing Apple's sandbox billing during beta

Health Data (With Your Permission)

If you grant HealthKit access, we may access:

  • Activity Data: Steps, active calories, exercise minutes
  • Body Measurements: Weight, height
  • Vital Signs: Heart rate, resting heart rate
  • Sleep Data: Sleep duration
  • Nutrition Data: Calories consumed

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: iOS version, device model, app version
  • Diagnostics: Crash reports and technical diagnostics to improve app stability

How We Use Your Information

Primary Uses

All data we collect is used solely to provide and improve app functionality:

  • AI Coaching: Generate personalized wellness coaching responses
  • Conversation Continuity: Maintain context across sessions so the AI remembers your history
  • Usage Management: Track turn counts to manage service limits
  • Service Improvement: Fix bugs and improve the app experience

AI Processing

Your data is processed by AI services to generate coaching responses:

  • Models Used: GPT-3.5-Turbo (for routing) and GPT-4o-mini (for responses)
  • Provider: OpenAI
  • What's Sent: Your messages, health metrics, and conversation context
  • Not Used for Training: Per OpenAI's API data usage policy, data sent via their API is not used to train their public AI models

Data Storage

Where Your Data Is Stored

Data Type Storage
User ID Cloud servers (AWS)
Conversation ID Cloud servers (AWS)
Conversation History Cloud servers (AWS)
Conversation Summaries Cloud servers (AWS)
Entitlement State Cloud servers (AWS)
Turn Counts Cloud servers (AWS)
Raw Health Metrics Processed temporarily; not permanently stored

Data Retention

  • Conversation Data: Retained to provide ongoing coaching continuity
  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Raw Health Data: Processed in real-time for AI responses; not permanently stored
  • Derived Insights: Summaries or insights derived from health data may be retained as part of conversation history

Data Security

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Data is stored on AWS infrastructure
  • Beta Status: As pre-release software, security features are still under development. We implement reasonable measures to protect your data.

Health Data Handling

Apple HealthKit Integration

  • We follow Apple's HealthKit privacy requirements
  • You control which health data types to share via iOS Settings
  • You can revoke health data access at any time
  • Health data is only accessed when you actively use the app

How Health Data Flows

  1. You ask a health-related question
  2. App retrieves relevant metrics from HealthKit (e.g., steps, weight, sleep)
  3. Health metrics are sent to our AI service along with your question
  4. AI generates a coaching response
  5. Response is returned to you
  6. Raw health metrics are not permanently stored on our servers

What We Store vs. What We Don't

  • Not Stored: Raw HealthKit metrics are processed temporarily and not permanently stored
  • May Be Stored: Derived insights or summaries may be retained as part of your conversation history to provide continuity

What We Do NOT Do

  • ❌ No Data Selling: We do not sell your personal information
  • ❌ No Advertising: We do not display ads or share data with advertisers
  • ❌ No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: We do not use analytics tools that track you
  • ❌ No Cross-App Tracking: We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • ❌ No AI Training: Data sent to AI services is not used to train public AI models

Third-Party Services

OpenAI

  • Purpose: AI model provider for generating coaching responses
  • Data Shared: Your messages, health metrics, conversation context
  • Their Policy: OpenAI's API data usage policy applies
  • Not Used for Training: Per OpenAI's policy, API data is not used to train their public models

Apple (HealthKit)

  • Purpose: Source of health data with your permission
  • Their Policy: Apple's privacy policy applies to HealthKit data

Amazon Web Services (AWS)

  • Purpose: Cloud infrastructure for data storage and processing
  • Their Policy: AWS's privacy and security policies apply

Your Rights and Choices

Data Control

  • Access: You can request information about what data we have
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and data
  • HealthKit Permissions: Manage in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > AskHealth AI
