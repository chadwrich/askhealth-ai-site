6 Core Health Fundamentals

AskHealth AI analyzes these key wellness metrics from your Apple Health data:

🔥 Total Calories

Energy expenditure tracking

Sum of active calories (from exercise) plus at-rest calories (basal metabolic rate). Understand your complete daily energy burn for better wellness planning.

Wellness insight, not medical advice

🍎 Dietary Calories

Food intake analysis

Track calories consumed from food and beverages. Get insights on energy balance and nutrition patterns to support your wellness goals.

Educational guidance only

⚖️ Weight

Progress tracking

Monitor weight trends over time. Understand patterns and get coaching on healthy approaches to weight management and body composition.

Wellness coaching, not medical diagnosis

👟 Steps

Daily activity monitoring

Track daily step counts and movement patterns. Get motivated with insights based on WHO recommendations (7,000-10,000+ steps daily).

General wellness guidelines

💤 Sleep Hours

Recovery and rest analysis

Monitor sleep duration and patterns. Receive insights based on sleep guidelines (7-9 hours for adults) to support recovery and wellness.

Educational information only

🏃‍♂️ Active Calories

Exercise intensity tracking

Monitor calories burned through physical activity and exercise. Get insights on meeting CDC activity guidelines (150+ minutes/week moderate activity).

Fitness guidance, not medical prescription

  • Revoke anytime: Change permissions through iOS Settings
  • No permanent storage: Raw health data processed temporarily only

Seamless Integration

  • Automatic data sync from Apple Health app
  • Works with all HealthKit-compatible devices and apps
  • No manual data entry required
  • Real-time insights from your actual health data

🎯 Our Wellness Approach

Safe Guidelines Used

  • FDA nutrition ranges: 10-35% protein recommendations
  • CDC activity guidelines: 150+ minutes/week moderate activity
  • WHO step recommendations: 7,000-10,000+ daily steps
  • Sleep guidelines: 7-9 hours for adults
  • AHA heart rate zones: 220-age formula for reference

Important: These are general wellness guidelines, not personalized medical recommendations.
