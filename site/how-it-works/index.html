How AskHealth AI Works

Your privacy-first wellness coaching experience

Important: AskHealth AI provides wellness coaching only, not medical advice. For medical emergencies, call 911 immediately.

Simple 3-Step Process

Step 1: Connect Your Health Data

With your permission, AskHealth AI connects to Apple HealthKit to access your health metrics including:

  • Daily steps and activity levels
  • Sleep duration and patterns
  • Weight and body measurements
  • Calories consumed and burned
  • Heart rate data
Privacy-First: You control exactly which data types to share. You can revoke access anytime through iOS Settings.

Step 2: AI Analysis

Our AI analyzes your wellness patterns to provide personalized insights:

  • Identifies trends in your activity and sleep
  • Compares your metrics to healthy ranges
  • Looks for opportunities to improve wellness
  • Generates coaching recommendations

Note: All analysis focuses on general wellness principles, not your specific medical history.

Step 3: Personalized Coaching

Get actionable wellness insights through conversational AI:

  • Ask questions about your health data
  • Receive personalized coaching responses
  • Get motivation and encouragement
  • Learn about healthy lifestyle choices

Remember: This is educational wellness coaching, not medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

Privacy & Security

Your Data Stays Private

  • Apple HealthKit Integration: Built with Apple's privacy-first health platform
  • No Data Selling: We never sell your personal health information
  • Secure Processing: Health data is processed temporarily and not permanently stored
  • Your Control: Manage permissions anytime through iOS Settings

Medical Disclaimer Reinforcement

AskHealth AI is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for:

  • Medical advice and recommendations
  • Diagnosis of health conditions
  • Treatment decisions
  • Medication changes
  • Any health concerns or symptoms
