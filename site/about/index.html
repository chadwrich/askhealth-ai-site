About AskHealth AI

Building privacy-first wellness coaching for everyone

🎯 Our Mission

To democratize personalized wellness coaching through AI, while maintaining the highest standards of privacy and safety.

We believe everyone deserves access to intelligent health insights that respect their privacy and empower their wellness journey.

🌟 Our Vision

We envision a world where:

  • Privacy comes first: Your health data remains under your complete control
  • Wellness is accessible: AI-powered coaching is available to everyone, not just the privileged few
  • Safety is paramount: Clear boundaries between wellness coaching and medical advice
  • Technology serves humanity: AI enhances human health without replacing human judgment

🛠️ Our Development Approach

🔒 Privacy by Design

Built from the ground up with Apple HealthKit integration, ensuring your data never leaves your control without explicit permission.

🛡️ Safety First

Comprehensive safety guardrails ensure we stay within wellness coaching boundaries and never provide medical advice.

📊 Real Data Focus

Every feature is tested with actual health data, not mock data. "Should work" doesn't equal "does work" in our development process.

🔄 Iterative Improvement

Beta testing with real users helps us build a product that truly serves your wellness needs.

🔐 Privacy-First Philosophy

Our commitment to your privacy isn't just a feature—it's our foundation:

What We Do

  • ✅ Respect your choices: You control which data to share
  • ✅ Transparent processing: Clear explanations of how your data is used
  • ✅ Minimal data retention: Raw health data processed temporarily, not stored
  • ✅ No data selling: Your information is never sold to third parties
  • ✅ Open communication: Clear privacy policies and terms

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ No hidden data collection: No tracking without your knowledge
  • ❌ No advertising model: No ads means no incentive to misuse your data
  • ❌ No medical overreach: We stay within wellness coaching boundaries
  • ❌ No data lock-in: You can export or delete your data anytime

🏗️ Built on Proven Foundations

AskHealth AI leverages established, trusted technologies:

  • Apple HealthKit: Industry-leading health data platform with privacy built-in
  • OpenAI API: Advanced AI models with data usage policies that protect your privacy
  • AWS Infrastructure: Enterprise-grade security and reliability
  • Evidence-based guidelines: FDA, CDC, WHO, and AHA recommendations as reference points

We stand on the shoulders of giants to deliver reliable, trustworthy wellness coaching.

🎯 Our Wellness Scope

What AskHealth AI provides:

  • Personalized wellness coaching and insights
  • Educational health information
  • Motivation and encouragement for healthy habits
  • Analysis of your health data trends
  • General fitness and nutrition guidance

What we explicitly do NOT provide:

  • Medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment
  • Emergency medical services
  • Prescription or medication recommendations
  • Replacement for healthcare professionals
  • Medical device functionality
⚠️ Important: AskHealth AI is designed for wellness coaching and educational purposes only. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical advice.
